How to order

Message us, pick a time slot, and we do the rest. Here is how every order works.

Start on WhatsApp

  1. Message us your order. WhatsApp, DM us on Instagram @cravealicious1, or call or text 07999 660309. Snapchat: cravealicious.

  2. Select a time slot for collection or delivery and confirm your order details.

  3. We confirm your order and prepare it to be ready within your selected time slot.

  4. Delivery orders are given a timeframe, and payment is taken in advance.

  5. Collection orders can be paid in advance or on collection.Collection orders are made on arrival unless paid in advance.

  6. Cash or card accepted. Please have exact cash.

Enjoy your order!
